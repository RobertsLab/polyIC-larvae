### Cycle quantity (Cq) data

These are exported from the [source `.pcrd` files](../cfx_files/). Contain plate layout, individual and mean Cqs, and standard deviations.

Info on primers can be found by referencing the SR ID in the Roberts Lab [`PrimerDatabase`](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/14m2kkFhxcoKWWIGoAD_7VOVsAg9wilME2UcSLqfnqLI/edit?usp=sharing) (Google Sheet).
