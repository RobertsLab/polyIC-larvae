### qPCR `.pcrd` (BioRad) files.

These are the source files and require CFX Maestro (BioRad) software to open. Some are paired with their exported Cq results CSV (also mirrored in [`../Cq/`](../Cq/)).

Files were generated on two instruments (indicated in the filename): `Connect` (CFX Connect) and `CFX96`. There are three plates per target (`-01`, `-02`, `-03`).

---

- `*cGAS*`: cGAS. SRIDs 1826/7.
- `*DNMT1*`: DNMT1. SRIDs 1510/1.
- `*citrate_synthase*`: citrate synthase. SRIDs 1383/4.
- `*ATP_synthase*`: ATP synthase. SRIDs 1385/6.
- `*GAPDH*`: GAPDH. SRIDs 1172/3.
- `*HSP70*`: HSP70. SRIDs 598/9.
- `*HSP90*`: HSP90. SRIDs 1532/3.

For more specific information on primers used for each target, reference the SR ID in the Roberts Lab [`PrimerDatabase`](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/14m2kkFhxcoKWWIGoAD_7VOVsAg9wilME2UcSLqfnqLI/edit?usp=sharing) (Google Sheet).
